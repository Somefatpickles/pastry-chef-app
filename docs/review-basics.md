# Recipe review: quick bakes, bars, cookies, and tarts

Reviewed October 24, 2026. Coverage: the 22 original recipe IDs from `banana-bread` through `palmiers` (original library positions 47–68).

This is an editorial and formula review using published baking references. These revised app formulas have **not** been baked in this review. Sources support techniques, plausible ratios, timing ranges, and doneness cues; where the app retains or adjusts its own formula, it is not represented as the publisher's exact tested recipe. Instructions are written independently. A physical bake of each version is still the appropriate acceptance test for flavor, actual yield, and the home's ovens and ingredients.

Every recipe now supplies a pan or portion size, equipment, a complete measured ingredient list, a specific oven setting where relevant, a timed method, sensory doneness checks, cooling or setting, storage, explanatory `why` text for every step, and linked research references in the app. Temperatures are conventional oven settings, with Fahrenheit and Celsius. Quantities are primarily weights so the app can scale them consistently; moisture-sensitive ingredients specify their condition.

## Practical validation still needed

The automated data checks confirm 22 unique recipe IDs, 144 explained method steps, numeric ingredient amounts, source links, equipment, storage, and review notes. They do not establish a successful physical bake. Priority home tests are the rebalanced oatmeal and peanut-butter cookies, the chewy blondie, the oil/cocoa crinkle, all four tart shells/fillings, and the loaf cakes' actual center timing. Record pan material, oven thermometer reading, ingredient brands, portion weights, actual yields, and sensory outcome before claiming the formulas are kitchen validated.

Scaled recipes should keep the indicated portion size and batter depth, distributing extra dough or batter among more pans. Times and temperatures should not be multiplied with ingredient quantities. Fractional eggs must be beaten and weighed. These are recipe-method requirements, not proof that every substitution or arbitrary batch size has been tested.
