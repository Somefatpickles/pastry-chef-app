# Recipe review: custards, choux, frostings, and everyday cakes

Reviewed October 24, 2026. This is an editorial and formula review of the 23 recipes originally numbered 24–46. It compares the app's original ingredients and methods with established baking references, then supplies independently written, complete instructions. These revised app formulas have **not been kitchen tested**. The references establish techniques and useful comparison formulas; they do not certify the app's independently adjusted quantities or every oven's timing.

All 23 revised recipes retain their original recipe IDs, titles, categories, and difficulty levels. Each now includes measured ingredients, equipment, timed methods, a reason behind every step, storage, notes, and linked research. Oven settings are conventional; temperature, timing, and visible doneness cues work together. Ingredient and step IDs were regenerated to avoid carrying old completion ticks onto materially revised methods.

## Limits and practical validation

The ingredients and methods were checked for internal consistency, complete named components, plausible ratios, heat settings, pan capacity, active/resting time distinctions, and storage. Research does not substitute for a practical bake. The family's next useful check is to bake representative recipes, recording actual pan, oven type, ingredient weights, observed bake time, and final texture. Prioritize the newly balanced chocolate cakes, the resized vanilla cake, the choux variants, and the revised scone dough.

Scaling should retain the given item size and pan depth by using more units or pans. Oven temperatures, proof cues, chill requirements, hot-oil depth, rolling thickness, and doneness targets are not linear quantities. Fractional eggs should be beaten and weighed; texture-dependent choux additions still stop at the stated consistency.
