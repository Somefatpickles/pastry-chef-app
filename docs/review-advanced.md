# Advanced recipe editorial review

Reviewed October 24, 2026. Scope: the original library's first 23 recipes, from carrot cake through chocolate mousse. The reviewed data is in `src/recipes/advanced.ts`.

This is a research-based editorial and formulation review, not kitchen validation. Sources were opened during the review. They support the cited techniques, component structure, and cooking endpoints; these independently written, sometimes reformulated app recipes have not been baked or laboratory tested. Home ovens, flour, chocolate percentage, cream fat, egg size, and equipment can change results. Use the doneness cues alongside the time ranges. Family bake-testing and feedback remain necessary before describing these as tested recipes.

The original recipes frequently had plausible ingredient lists but insufficient operational detail. Some advanced entries were only assembly outlines with purchased or unexplained components. The mousse entries presented a more serious issue: one used uncooked yolks without a cooking step, while the other referred to an undefined yolk mixture. All 23 now have measured ingredients, equipment and pan dimensions, chronological instructions, a reason behind each step, total elapsed-time estimates, storage, source links, and a review note.

## Food handling and scaling

- Egg-containing chilled fillings, whipped cream cakes, and cheese pastries require prompt refrigeration at 40°F / 4°C or colder. The app records a two-hour maximum at room temperature, reduced to one hour above 90°F / 32°C. [FDA egg safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety).
- Heated meringue and mousse custard have a thermometer endpoint. Browning only the outside of a meringue does not cook the center.
- Purchased all-butter puff pastry, commercial praline paste, and ice cream are intentional measured ingredients. Other required components now have complete methods in their own recipe.
- Numeric batch scaling multiplies ingredients. Pan sizes, lamination thickness, layer dimensions, oven temperatures, and doneness cues remain those of the base method. Make additional batches/pans at the original depth; do not put 2.5 times the batter into one base pan. For fractional eggs, beat and weigh the required fraction.
- Water-bath water and knife-greasing oil are process allowances and carry `scaleWithBatch: false`.
- Allergen statements identify the supplied formulas; they are not guarantees about brands, shared equipment, or AI-proposed substitutions.
