# Recipe review — October 24, 2026

All 68 original recipes were reviewed individually for ingredient balance, omitted components, method completeness, temperatures, timing, pan capacity, doneness, handling and storage. Full findings and supporting references are recorded in the three audit files below and embedded in each recipe.

| Group | Recipes | Ingredient lines | Explained steps | Detailed audit |

| Layer cakes, advanced pastries, yeast bakes and custards | 23 | 277 | 193 | [Advanced review](review-advanced.md) |
| Creams, choux, frostings, quick bakes and cakes | 23 | 221 | 152 | [Middle review](review-middle.md) |
| Loaves, bars, cookies and tarts | 22 | 196 | 144 | [Basics review](review-basics.md) |

Representative corrections include missing complete sponge/filling formulas in assembled cakes, cooked-yolk handling for chocolate mousse, quantified lamination and proofing, complete choux fillings and drying schedules, specified custard and meringue temperatures, accurate pan sizes, measured streusel and lemon filling flour, and the missing dry-ingredient step in snickerdoodles.

No recipe is marked kitchen tested. Published references support technique and ratio review; independently reformulated variants still need a physical bake. Baking times depend on the actual oven, pan material, batch depth and portion size. The app supplies a checking range plus practical doneness cues rather than promising a single exact endpoint.

The AI chef's proposals are additional experimental adaptations. Library source links are retained as background; they do not validate a proposed substitute formula. Apply changes only after reviewing the proposal and its considerations, then save a separate local copy if desired.

Software validation and any rendered/device-testing limits are recorded in [validation.md](validation.md). Current publication and deployment details are recorded in [release notes](release-notes.md).
